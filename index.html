Case study / 001

An honest field instrument.

The Expedition Metal Field is a study in restraint. Its brushed steel case is broad enough to protect the dial, yet slim enough to disappear under a cuff. A warm ivory face carries full Arabic numerals, a date window and a second time scale.

The leather is cut with a subtle taper, double stitched at the edges and stamped with the Expedition mark. Soft key lighting pulls the grain and brushed metal into relief.
